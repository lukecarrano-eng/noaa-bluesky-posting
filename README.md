# How Do NOAA's Bluesky Accounts Differ in What and When They Post?

**Author:** Luke Carrano

## Summary

NOAA runs several verified accounts on Bluesky under its own .gov domain: the main agency account, the National Weather Service, NOAA Climate, and NOAA Communications. This project asks how these accounts differ in how often they post, which days and hours they post, and how much they rely on images and video, and whether those differences line up with how many reposts their posts get. I collect the public posts from the Bluesky API and compare the accounts.
